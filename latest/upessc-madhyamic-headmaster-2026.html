UPESSC UP Madhyamic Headmaster Recruitment 2026 Online Form

Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj dwara Uttar Pradesh Headmaster Examination 2026 ke tahat total 1,172 posts par recruitment ke liye online aavedan amantrit kiye gaye hain. Eligible candidates 23 October 2026 se 22 November 2026 tak apply kar sakte hain.

📅 Important Dates (महत्वपूर्ण तिथियां)
Application Begin: 23/10/2026
Last Date for Registration: 22/11/2026
Pay Exam Fee Last Date: 22/11/2026
Last Date Correction: 25/11/2026
UPESSC Headmaster 2026 Exam Date: 07/01/2027
Admit Card Available: Before Exam

💳 Application Fee

  • • General / OBC / EWS: ₹1500/-
  • • SC / ST: ₹1500/-
  • • PH (Divyang): ₹1500/-
  • Mode: Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, UPI fee mode only.

⏳ Age Limit (As on 01/07/2026)

  • • Minimum Age: 30 Years
  • • Maximum Age: 62 Years
  • • Age Relaxation as per Uttar Pradesh UPESSC Headmaster Examination Rules.

🎓 Eligibility & Vacancy Details (Total: 1,172 Posts)

Headmaster (Balak): 1,037 Posts

Master Degree with Minimum 50% Marks + B.Ed / B.P.Ed Exam Passed + 4 Year Experience.

Headmaster (Balika): 135 Posts

Master Degree with Minimum 50% Marks + B.Ed / B.P.Ed Exam Passed + 4 Year Experience.

📝 How to Fill UPESSC UP Headmaster Online Form 2026

  • 1. Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj ne Headmaster Examination 2026 ka notification jari kiya hai. Candidate 23/10/2026 se 22/11/2026 ke beech apply kar sakte hain.
  • 2. Application form apply karne se pehle official notification dhyan se padhein.
  • 3. Sabhi documents jaise Eligibility, ID Proof, Address Details, aur Basic Details check karke collect kar lein.
  • 4. Scan kiye hue documents (Photo, Signature, ID Proof, etc.) ready rakhein.
  • 5. Form submit karne se pehle preview aur sabhi columns ko dhyan se check kar lein.
  • 6. Application fee pay karna anivarya hai, bina fee ke form complete nahi mana jayega.
  • 7. Final submitted form ka print out nikal kar surakshit rakh lein.
