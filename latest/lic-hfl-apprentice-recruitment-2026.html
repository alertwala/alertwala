LIC Housing Finance Limited (LIC HFL) Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Online Form

LIC Housing Finance Limited (LIC HFL) dwara desh bhar ke various offices mein fresh graduates ke liye Apprenticeship Program ke tahat total 232 posts par recruitment ke liye notification jari kiya gaya hai. Eligible candidates NATS portal ke madhyam se apply kar sakte hain.

📅 Important Dates (महत्वपूर्ण तिथियां)
Application Opening Date: 20/10/2026
Last Date to Pay Exam Fee (BFSI SSC): 01/11/2026
Online Proctored Exam Date: 07/11/2026
Document Verification & Interview: 15/11/2026

💳 Application Fee

  • • SC / ST / PwBD Candidates: ₹200/- + GST
  • • General, EWS & OBC Male: ₹800/- + GST
  • • General, EWS & OBC Female / Transgender: ₹600/- + GST
  • Mode: Online via BFSI SSC Portal (beep.bfsissc.com)

⏳ Age Limit (As on 01/10/2026)

  • • Minimum Age: 20 Years
  • • Maximum Age: 25 Years
  • • Age relaxation rules ke anusar extra.
