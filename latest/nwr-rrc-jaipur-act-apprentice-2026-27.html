North Western Railway (NWR) RRC Jaipur Act Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 Online Form

Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), North Western Railway, Jaipur dwara Act Apprentice Act 1961 ke tahat various Workshops aur Units mein total 2,008 training slots ke liye revised notification jari kiya gaya hai. Eligible candidates official website www.rrcjaipur.in ke madhyam se apply kar sakte hain.

📅 Important Dates (महत्वपूर्ण तिथियां)
Revised Notification Date: 21/10/2026
Online Application Start Date: 23/10/2026
Online Application Last Date & Time: 22/11/2026 (23:59 Hrs)

💳 Application Fee

  • • General / OBC / EWS: ₹100/-
  • • SC / ST / PwBD / Female Candidates: ₹0/- (Nil)
  • Mode: Online (Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, UPI)

⏳ Age Limit (As on 22/11/2026)

  • • Minimum Age: 15 Years
  • • Maximum Age: 24 Years
  • • Age Relaxation: SC/ST (5 Yrs), OBC (3 Yrs), PwBD (10 Yrs) extra as per rules.

🎓 Educational & Technical Qualification

Academic Qualification: Kisi recognized board se minimum 50% marks ke sath 10th class (Matriculation) ya iske समकक्ष pass honi chahiye.

Technical Qualification: Related trade mein NCVT / SCVT se recognized ITI Pass certificate hona anivarya hai.
