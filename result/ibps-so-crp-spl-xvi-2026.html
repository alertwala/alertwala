Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) Specialist Officer SO CRP SPL-XVI Recruitment 2026

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) dwara CRP SPL-XVI notification jari ki gayi hai. Is countrywide recruitment process ke tahat participating banks mein IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR, aur Marketing Officer (Scale-I) ke posts bhare jayenge. Eligible candidates 01 July 2026 se 21/26 July 2026 tak apply kar sakte hain.

📅 Important Dates (महत्वपूर्ण तिथियां)
Online Application Start Date: 01-07-2026
Online Application Last Date: 26-07-2026
Fee Payment Last Date: 26-07-2026
Online Preliminary Exam Date: August 2026
Prelims Result Declared: 15 October 2026
Online Main Exam Date: 01 November 2026
Interview Date: November / December 2026
Provisional Allotment: January 2027

💳 Application Fee

  • • General / OBC / EWS: ₹ 850/-
  • • SC / ST / PwBD: ₹ 175/-
  • Mode: Online Mode Only (Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, UPI, etc.)

⏳ Post Details & Age Limit (Scale-I)

  • • Age Limit: 20 to 30 Years (As per rules).
  • • Posts Included: IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR/Personnel Officer, Marketing Officer.

🎓 Educational Eligibility (Cut-off date: 21/07/2026)

  • IT Officer (Scale-I): 4-year Engineering Degree (B.E/B.Tech) in CS/IT/Electronics/Telecommunication OR Post Graduation (M.Sc/MCA/M.Tech) in relevant subject OR DOEACC 'B' Level pass.
  • Agricultural Field Officer (Scale-I): 4-year Graduation Degree in Agriculture / Horticulture / Animal Husbandry / Dairy Science / Fishery Science / Forestry / Agri-Business Management, etc.
  • Rajbhasha Adhikari (Scale-I): Post Graduation Degree in Hindi with English as a subject at graduation level, or Sanskrit with Hindi and English at graduation.
  • Law Officer (Scale-I): Bachelor Degree in Law (LL.B) and enrolled as an advocate with Bar Council.
  • HR/Personnel Officer (Scale-I): Graduate with 2-year full-time PG Degree or PG Diploma in Personnel Management / Industrial Relations / HR / Social Work / Labour Law.
  • Marketing Officer (Scale-I): Graduate with 2-year full-time MMS (Marketing) / MBA (Marketing) / PGDBA / PGDBM with marketing specialization.

📋 Exam Pattern & Selection Process

  • • Prelims Exam: Qualifying nature, consisting of English, Reasoning, and Quantitative Aptitude/General Awareness sections (Total 100 questions, 125 marks, 80 minutes).
  • • Main Exam: Professional Knowledge test based on the respective field.
  • • Interview: Candidates shortlisted from Mains will appear for a Common Interview (Weightage ratio 80:20 for Mains and Interview).
  • • Negative Marking: 0.25 marks deduction for every wrong answer in both Prelims and Mains.
