SBI Junior Associate (Clerk) Backlog Special Recruitment Drive 2026 Online Form

State Bank of India (SBI) dwara Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) – Special Recruitment Drive for SC/ST/OBC Backlog Vacancies ke tahat total 1,538 vacancies par recruitment ke liye online aavedan amantrit kiye gaye hain. Eligible candidates 07 August 2026 se 27 August 2026 tak apply kar sakte hain.

📅 Important Dates (महत्वपूर्ण तिथियां)
Online Application Start Date: 07/08/2026
Online Application Last Date: 27/08/2026
Fee Payment Last Date: 27/08/2026
Prelims Exam Date: 16 September 2026
Mains Exam Date: October / November 2026 (Tentative)

💳 Application Fee

  • • OBC (Non Creamy Layer): ₹750/-
  • • SC / ST / PwBD / ESM: ₹0/- (Nil / निःशुल्क)
  • Mode: Online via Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, UPI.

⏳ Age Limit (As on 01/04/2026)

  • • Minimum Age: 20 Years
  • • Maximum Age: 28 Years (Born between 02.04.1998 and 01.04.2006)
  • • Age Relaxation: SC/ST (5 Yrs), OBC (3 Yrs), PwBD (10-15 Yrs) as per rules.

📊 Category Wise Vacancies

  • • SC: 207 Posts
  • • ST: 1,219 Posts
  • • OBC: 112 Posts
  • • Total Backlog Vacancies: 1,538 Posts

🎓 Educational Qualification & Proficiency

  • Graduation: Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University or Institute.
  • Appearing Students: Final year/semester students can apply provided their result is declared on or before 31.12.2026.
  • Local Language Proficiency: Candidates must be proficient in reading, writing, speaking, and understanding the specified local language of the applied state/UT.

📋 Exam Pattern Overview

  • • Prelims Exam: 100 Objective Questions for 100 Marks (English, Numerical Ability, Reasoning Ability). Duration: 60 Minutes.
  • • Mains Exam: 190 Questions for 200 Marks (Financial Awareness, General English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning & Computer Aptitude). Duration: 2 Hours 40 Minutes.
  • • Negative Marking: 1/4th (0.25 marks) deduction for every incorrect answer.
