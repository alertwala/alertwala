🔥 LNMU Darbhanga Session 2026-30 Spot Admission

LNMU UG Admission Spot Round 2026-30 (Graduation Spot Admission)

Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU), Darbhanga dwara graduation courses (Session 2026-30) mein vacant seats par admission ke liye Spot Round Admission ki official notice jari kar di gayi hai. Ye undergraduate courses mein admission lene ka final mauka hai. Eligible candidates SAMARTH portal ke madhyam se apply kar sakte hain.

📅 Important Dates (महत्वपूर्ण तिथियां)
Official Notice Date: 05/10/2026
Spot Admission Online Application Date: 06 – 08 October 2026
Merit List / Selected Candidates List Date: 09 October 2026
College Admission Date (नामांकन तिथि): 10 – 12 October 2026

💳 Application Fee Details

  • • Pehle se registered candidates (Already Registered): ₹0/- (No extra fee)
  • • New applicants (Naye abhyarthi jinhone abhi tak aavedan nahi kiya): ₹700/-
  • Mode: Online via SAMARTH Portal.

📌 Spot Admission Eligibility & Guidelines

  • • **Pehle se registered candidates:** Jinka admission pehle ke rounds mein nahi ho saka, ve apni login ID se login karke bina kisi extra fee ke max 3 colleges aur 5 subjects ka naya option chun sakte hain.
  • • **Naye candidates:** Jinhone session 2026-30 ke liye pehle apply nahi kiya tha, ve ₹700 fee ke sath max 3 colleges aur 5 subjects chun kar naya aavedan kar sakte hain.
  • • **Apatrata (Not Eligible):** Jinka admission pehle hi kisi college mein ho chuka hai, ve spot admission ke liye eligible nahi honge.
  • • **Seats Availability:** Spot admission kewal available vacant seats ke khilaf hoga. Vacancy details LNMU portal par dekhi ja sakti hai.

📝 How to Apply for LNMU UG Spot Admission 2026

  • 1. Sabse pehle official portal lnmuadm.samarth.edu.in ya lnmu.ac.in par jayein.
  • 2. Portal par available college-wise aur subject-wise vacant seats ki list check karein.
  • 3. Registered students apne purane Login ID & Password se login karke 3 colleges aur 5 subjects select karein.
  • 4. New students 'New Registration' par click karke details bharein aur ₹700 online application fee pay karein.
  • 5. Form review karne ke baad final submit karein aur hard copy download kar lein.
  • 6. 09 October 2026 ko merit list aane ke baad 10 se 12 October 2026 tak allot kiye gaye college mein admission lein.
