🔥 Education Department, Government of Bihar Session 2026-27

Bihar Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) Session 2026-27 Online Form

Bihar sarkar ke Education Department dwara state ke SC, ST, BC aur EBC category ke students ke liye Bihar Post Matric Scholarship (PMS) Session 2026-27 ka online application portal 15 July 2026 se shuru kiya ja raha hai. Eligible students official portal ke madhyam se apply kar sakte hain.

📅 Important Dates (महत्वपूर्ण तिथियां)
Official Notice Date: 02/07/2026
Online Application Starting Date: 15/07/2026
Online Application Last Date: 30/09/2026
Physical Verification at Institution Level: Along with applications

💰 Estimated Scholarship Amount (Per Year)

Sl. No. Course Details / Class Estimated Assistance Amount
1 Intermediate (11th & 12th – Arts, Science, Commerce) ₹ 2,000/- to ₹ 4,000/-
2 Graduation Level (BA, BSc, BCom, etc.) ₹ 5,000/- to ₹ 8,000/-
3 Technical & Professional Courses (ITI, Diploma, Polytechnic) Up to ₹ 10,000/-
4 Higher Professional Technical Courses (B.Tech, MBBS, MBA, etc.) ₹ 15,000/- to ₹ 40,000/-

📋 Eligibility & Criteria

  • • Domicile: Applicant must be a permanent resident of Bihar state.
  • • Caste Category: Scheme is only for SC, ST, BC, and EBC category students (General category students are not eligible).
  • • Educational Qualification: Minimum 10th (Matric) passed and currently studying in higher classes.
  • • Admission 2026: Valid admission in Session 2026-27 for 11th, 12th, Graduation (Part 1/2/3), or Professional courses (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Semester).
  • • Family Income: Total annual income of parents from all sources must not exceed ₹ 2,00,000/- (Two Lakh Rupees).

📄 Required Documents List

  • • Caste Certificate: Digitally signed caste certificate issued by competent authority in Bihar.
  • • Residential Certificate: Valid residential certificate proving permanent residency in Bihar.
  • • Income Certificate: Fresh income certificate for Session 2026-27 showing family income under ₹2 Lakhs.
  • • Bonafide Certificate: Issued on official letterhead of the school/college with principal's signature & seal.
  • • Aadhaar Seeded Bank Account: Single bank account in student's name, mandatorily Aadhaar Seeded (DBT Enabled).
  • • Additional Docs: 10th/12th/Graduation Marksheets, Aadhaar Card, Fee Receipt, and Passport Size Photo.

🔍 Verification Process

  1. Institution Verification: Nodal officer of respective school/college verifies bonafide and fee receipt online.
  2. Physical Verification Committee: District-level team verifies actual admission physically at the college.
  3. District Welfare Officer (DWO Approval): Final approval by DWO after successful verification from both levels.
  4. DBT Payment: Direct transfer to student's Aadhaar-seeded bank account via PFMS system.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Bihar Post Matric Scholarship Session 2026-27 ke liye aavedan kab se shuru hoga?
Ans: Online application process 15 July 2026 se official portal par shuru ki jayegi.

Q2: Is scholarship ke liye kaun-kaun se students patra hain?
Ans: Bihar state ke SC, ST, BC aur EBC category ke students jinhone session 2026-27 mein 11th, 12th, graduation ya various semesters mein admission liya hai.

Q3: Aavedan karne ke liye kaun se mukhya documents taiyar rakhne honge?
Ans: Residential, Income, Caste certificate, Bonafide certificate aur Aadhaar seeded bank account.

Q4: Kya joint bank account ya parents ke account mein scholarship aa sakti hai?
Ans: Nahi, student ka bank account single hona chahiye aur mandatory roop se Aadhaar Seeded hona chahiye.
